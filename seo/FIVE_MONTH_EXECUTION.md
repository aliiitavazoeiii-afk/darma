# DARMA — five-month SEO & AI visibility operating plan

Start date for the **ranking clock**: the actual public, crawlable, indexable launch, NOT the date this plan was written. If launched 2026-11-05, four-five months ends ~2027-03-05 to 2027-04-05. If launch later, the measurement window slides accordingly.

## Mission and probability
Stretch goal: page-one placements for 3-5 selected, relevant NON-BRAND mid/long-tail commercial queries within 5 months from launch. Not a guarantee and not a prediction; assess feasibility after real SERP/competition/keyword research. Broad "خرید شورت زنانه" may require substantially longer. Secondary objectives: brand-entity clarity, product discovery, organic qualified sessions, measurable AI search impressions/citations and profitable sales.

## Before launch / baseline planning
1. Inventory taxonomy and exact source-of-truth catalog: parent garment model, pack size, sizes M-3XL, colors, actual material and measurements, SKU and stock, delivery and returns. Do not make "cotton/lace" categories without actual products.
2. Keyword research by query, intent, search volume estimate, competition and SERP format; record 20-30 priority terms with URL map. Split brand, transactional, informational and natural-language question queries.
3. Competitor audit of top-10 results for each primary SERP: product mix, page type, topical depth, image quality, UX and links; never copy text or branded artwork.
4. Validate production domain, payment and schema, copy, performance test, CDN robot access and preview-only environment.
5. Create/verify Search Console domain property, web analytics events, and eventual sitemap submission AFTER public deployment.
6. Define launch day baseline as zero/unknown until measured.

## Month 1 after launch — index and foundation
- Search Console indexed page coverage, sitemap, robots/CDN, 404/redirect/canonical, proper product schema and site name.
- Publish high-quality home + 3-5 primary catalog/category landing pages + every stocked SKU page with unique useful descriptions and real photography.
- Publish verified size guide and first 2 original materials/care guides.
- Test core web vitals/real Android + Iran network, product schema and mobile purchase journey.
- Output: coverage dashboard, 0 critical index blockers, valid primary structured data, first verified discovery & nonbrand impressions.

## Month 2 — topical depth
- Publish 3-4 original expert-reviewed articles around fit, fabric, care, packs and comfort only where first-hand detail is available.
- Improve internal links product <-> collection <-> guides; add visible FAQ where genuinely requested (not FAQ rich-result bait).
- Fix low-value titles/duplicate URLs; strengthen authentic brand trust and policies.
- Output: trend in index coverage and nonbrand impressions, initial long-tail query ranking distribution.

## Month 3 — authority & conversion
- Expand top-performing cluster from real GSC data; enrich pages lagging in CTR/conversion.
- Seek truthful earned mentions, supplier/brand partnerships and editorial links where relevant, never buying fake links.
- Re-check technical crawl, product feeds/Google Merchant Center eligibility (country/service constraints must be checked, not assumed), and page-level product markup.
- Output: sustained qualified clicks, fewer index anomalies, conversion baseline, first AI visibility audit.

## Month 4 — focus on near-page-one
- For target query groups in positions ~11-30, improve query-intent alignment, original photos and explanations, content completeness, snippet relevance, internal linking.
- Update accurate product prices, availability and season-specific collections without gratuitous date updates.
- Output: higher share of top-20/top-10 terms, organic product clickthrough and user buying behavior.

## Month 5 — validate and scale
- Evaluate reach: for selected nonbrand targets how many in top 10, stable for 2-4 weekly checks.
- Record GSC organic clicks, CTR, conversions/revenue, AI-feature impressions and ChatGPT referrals, across baseline and 3-month comparison windows.
- Decide next 90-day investments from evidence, not vanity AI scores.
- Ship rigorous technical & content maintenance schedule.

## Sample keyword clusters (not validated yet)
Commercial: خرید شورت زنانه, شورت زنانه دارما, پک شورت زنانه دارما, پک شورت زنانه سه تایی, پک شورت زنانه چهار تایی, پک شورت زنانه پنج تایی, پک شورت زنانه شش تایی.
Informational: راهنمای انتخاب سایز شورت زنانه; شورت زنانه نخی یا توری; روش شستن شورت زنانه; انتخاب پک چند عددی.
Brand: دارما, DARMA underwear, دارما لباس زیر.
Target queries, prioritization and exact category slugs depend on research and catalog reality.

## Weekly operating review
- Search Console: indexing coverage, clicks, impressions, CTR, average positions (segment branded/nonbranded).
- Generative AI Search Console report: impressions/pages where enough data exists.
- Manual repeatable check: 20-30 natural-language shopping questions in ChatGPT/other relevant search products; citation URL yes/no; observational only, not a guaranteed AI metric.
- Sales funnel: organic session -> product view -> add to cart -> checkout -> purchase and order value.
- SEO health: robots, 5xx, canonical regressions, Core Web Vitals, broken links, price/stock parity with schemas.
- Priority queue: critical crawler/checkout issues first, then high-intent landing pages, then publishing cadence.

## Practical quality targets (internal goals, not ranking factors)
- 100% of priority indexable pages in sitemap with consistent canonical.
- 0 deliberate misinformation, fake ratings or bogus offers.
- 0 critical Product JSON-LD errors on audited live product pages.
- LCP <= 2.5s, INP <= 200ms, CLS <= 0.1 at field p75 (when available).
- Lighthouse mobile >= 90 on key templates as an internal engineering budget, not a Google rank promise.
- Month-5 top-10 nonbrand stretch goal 3-5 terms, subject to SERP research and real launch timing.
